# Frozen feasibility protocol — 26 October 2026

This is a structural screening pilot, not a novelty-cleared full experiment or faithful ConANN/DARTH reproduction. DARTH+ full text remains inaccessible; that literature gate stays open. The earlier audit is superseded only to permit this bounded feasibility test. Generic calibration repairs are known, not original methods.

Question: does natural query-dependent output size cause a material recall-risk increase after separate fixed-k calibration, and does standard selector-specific calibration resolve it?

Data: ANN-Benchmarks GloVe-25 angular (public mirror), 20,000 sampled corpus vectors; official Fashion-MNIST raw images, 10,000 sampled training images, unit-scaled Euclidean. Fashion raw compressed files replace the identical image benchmark HDF5 after its incomplete download exhausted disk space; no performance was examined before this change. Recompute exact neighbors for each sampled corpus. Never use full-corpus ground truth. Query permutation seed 20261026: 400 design / 1,000 calibration / 600 pilot; remaining official queries reserved. Fashion gzip decompression necessarily reads archive bytes, but reserved query vectors are never transformed, traced or evaluated. Public query IDs are not secret; access separation is procedural, not cryptographic. Preserve all IDs and checksums.

CPU IVF, 64 clusters, MiniBatchKMeans with 3 restarts and 100 max iterations; index seeds 0..4; one numeric thread. k={1,10,50}, alpha=.10 mean FNR. All budgets 1..64. Exact distances within lists. Offline exact-neighbor traces yield recall and vector-distance counts; no online latency claim. Build and trace runtime recorded. Centroid overhead: 64 additional vector distances per query, common to all IVF methods. Full exact brute force need not pay that overhead.

Primary selector: k=50 when nearest/second-nearest centroid distance ratio exceeds the DESIGN median; k=1 otherwise. Prespecified robustness: 25th/75th percentile cuts and inverse median rule. Uniform-random k and each constant k are controls. No oracle recall used for query-time selection. The natural ambiguity rule is a plausible engineering proxy, not a validated downstream RAG policy.

Baselines: naive separate per-k CRC; exact-selector common-budget CRC; exact-selector scaled per-k-budget CRC; worst-over-k scaled-budget CRC; simultaneous per-k Hoeffding bound (delta=.05); exact search. CRC correction (sum(loss)+1)/(n+1)<=.10. Nested family and full-search fallback. Scale template fitted on DESIGN labels only; 129 fixed multipliers from 0 to 64, ceil and clip to 1..64. Calibration labels certify that independently fixed nested family. All baselines receive the same 1,000 calibration queries; none gets a pilot-driven tuning budget. Hoeffding has a different high-probability risk guarantee and must be labeled as such. Numeric epsilon/tie ordering deterministic.

Smoke: compare recall from traces to independently reconstructed brute-force candidate sets for every k/probe; verify monotone loss/work, exact full-search loss zero, CRC correction and fallback. Split disjointness checked during loading.

Primary opportunity gate, frozen before performance: primary naive-selector FNR>.13 and query-bootstrap lower 95% bound>.10 in >=4/5 seeds on BOTH datasets. A later distinct proposed solution must maintain .10 risk and save >=10% distances against the strongest applicable standard safe baseline, confirmed on fresh queries and another index family. Generic CRC alone never passes the novelty gate. Intervals: 1,000 query bootstrap resamples per index seed, conditional on fitted index/calibration. Shared queries mean seed runs are not independent replications. Across-seed means are descriptive only. Secondary selector results cannot replace the primary gate after inspection.

If this gate fails, stop this direction, preserve reserved data, and do not run full experiments or write a positive IEEE paper. If it passes, first resolve remaining literature and distinct-contribution questions, freeze confirmation, then evaluate another index family and independently collected queries. No confirmed discovery or paper is promised.

Implementation correction after pilot: simultaneous marginal bounds alone do not protect query-dependent k (the very question being tested). The Hoeffding control therefore bounds the per-query maximum loss, with a union bound over the design-fixed scaled-budget family, and exact fallback. This replaces the per-k Hoeffding comparator specified above; all ten pilot runs are regenerated. Primary selector, primary naive method, splits and opportunity gate are unchanged. This is a correctness repair, not outcome-driven tuning.
